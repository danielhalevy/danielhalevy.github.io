The Hourglass King - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026 (draft until published; set the date on the day it goes live)

App: The Hourglass King (Android, package com.hourglass.king)

Developer: Daniel Halevi, Israel. Contact: hourglasskingsupport@gmail.com

Audience: players aged 13 and over. Not directed at children under 13. Not part of Google Play's Families program.

1. The short version

  • I (the developer) run no server for this game and have no analytics, no user accounts of my own, no chat and no tracking in it.
  • Your progress and settings are stored on your phone.
  • Optional Google Play Games sign-in: if you sign in, your progress is also saved to your own Google account (Play Games "Saved Games"), and scores can go to Play Games leaderboards. The in-game Store asks you to sign in first.
  • Optional rewarded video ads (Google AdMob): shown only when you tap an ad button. Google collects some device data for them (section 4).
  • Optional purchases (chests, coin packs and other packs) go through Google Play. I never see your payment details.
  • Optional daily reminder notifications, made on your phone only.
  • I do not sell your personal data.

2. Who we are

The Hourglass King is made by Daniel Halevi, a solo developer in Israel. For privacy questions, deletion requests or complaints write to hourglasskingsupport@gmail.com.

3. What is stored or collected, feature by feature

3.1 Saves and settings (on your phone only)

Your save slots, chest and coin balances, Descent and mode progress, leaderboard bests, settings (volume, vibration, touch-button layout, Remove-ads switch and similar) and a small clock record (so daily rewards cannot be cheated by changing the phone clock) are files in the game's private storage. The developer cannot see them. They are deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its storage. Android may include app data in your own device backup under your Google account settings; I have no access to that backup.

3.2 Google Play Games (optional)

  • Sign-in. The game uses Google Play Games Services. If you sign in, Google tells the game your Play Games display name (shown in the game's cloud page). The game does not ask for your email address, phone number or real name.
  • Cloud save. If cloud save is on, one bundle of your save files is uploaded to Play Games Saved Games, which Google stores in your Google account. It is read back on a new phone or when you tap Restore. I cannot read it. It is a few kilobytes of game progress (no photos, contacts or device data).
  • Leaderboards and achievements. If you sign in, your best scores and some story-progress milestones are sent to Google Play Games, and the top scores of other players (with their Play Games names) are shown to you. Leaderboard entries follow Google's Play Games rules and your Play Games profile settings.
  • You can play the story without signing in. The in-game Store needs the sign-in so that purchases and progress can be restored.

3.3 Ads (Google AdMob, optional)

See section 4.

3.4 Purchases (Google Play Billing, optional)

See section 5.

3.5 Time check

To keep daily rewards fair, the game sometimes asks a Google web address (www.gstatic.com) for the current time. This is a plain HTTPS request with no body that carries no game data; Google, like any web server, sees your IP address and basic request details. It happens at start and when you return to the app (at most about every 10 minutes), even if you never use ads or sign in.

3.6 Reminders (notifications, optional)

The game can show daily reminders ("a prize or a boss is waiting"). They are scheduled on your phone with Android's alarm system and are never sent from a server. The game asks for the Android notification permission once, after your first daily chest, and you can refuse it or switch reminders off in Settings. The phone keeps the reminder texts and times so they can be set again after a restart (the game requests the "run at boot" permission for this). The game keeps a local count of the hours at which you usually play to choose reminder times; it stays on the phone.

3.7 Error report (only when you tap it)

The game keeps short rotating log files on your phone (build, Android version, device model, game events, freezes and error lines). Nothing is sent automatically. If you tap Report error, the game prepares a text file of these logs and opens your email app so you can send it to support; you see it before sending. Logs may also appear in Android's system log while the game runs.

3.8 Game updates, art packs and review prompt

The game uses Google Play services to check for updates, to download its extra art pack after install (Play Asset Delivery), and, once, to ask whether you want to rate the game (Play In-App Review). These are run by Google Play; the game receives only the success or progress status.

4. Advertising: Google AdMob and your choices

The game shows rewarded video ads only: nothing pops up by itself and nothing plays between rooms. An ad plays only after you tap a button such as "Watch an ad" (for example to continue after a defeat, a second chance, a double reward, a reroll, a hint or a little silver).

When an ad is requested, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and use: the Android advertising ID, your IP address and approximate location derived from it, device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version), ad interactions, and diagnostic and crash data from the SDK. Google uses this to deliver, measure and personalise ads and to prevent fraud. The developer receives only totals and reports from AdMob, not your personal data. The ads SDK is started only when an ad offer is first needed, and not at all while you have Settings > Remove ads turned on (except the shop's optional "Watch an ad" row, which starts it on demand).

Consent. Where the law requires it (for example the EEA, UK and Switzerland) the game shows Google's consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) before any ad is requested, and ads are not requested until consent allows it. You can change your choice later at Settings > Privacy choices (shown when your region requires it).

Your other choices. You can switch ads off in Settings > Remove ads (this is a setting, not a purchase), reset or delete your advertising ID or turn off ad personalisation in Android Settings > Privacy > Ads.

Content. Ad content is limited to the "G" (general audiences) rating and the game is not marked child-directed.

5. Purchases: Google Play Billing

Optional in-app purchases (chests, coin packs and starter, zone and Second Wind packs; the odds of chests are shown in the game before you buy) are handled entirely by Google Play. I never see your card, name or address. The game receives from Google Play a confirmation of the purchase (product ID and purchase token) and uses it on your phone to deliver what you bought and finish the purchase with Google Play; delivered purchase tokens are kept in your save files (and in your cloud save, so a purchase is never given twice). When the game starts it also asks Google Play which purchases are still unfinished so none is lost. Refunds follow Google Play's refund policy. See the Google Play Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

6. Permissions the game asks Android for

  • Internet and network state: ads, Google Play services, the time check.
  • Vibrate: optional vibration (can be turned off in Settings).
  • Google Play Billing (com.android.vending.BILLING): purchases.
  • Advertising ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID): added by the Google ads SDK.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) and run at boot (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): daily reminders only.
  • No camera, microphone, contacts, photos, files or precise location.

8. Children

The game is for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13 and is not in Google Play's Families program. I do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used the game, write to hourglasskingsupport@gmail.com and I will help you with deletion at Google.

9. Security

The game has no server of its own. Data sent to Google (ads, Play Games, Billing, time check) travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Local save files are written atomically to private app storage. No method is perfectly secure.

10. Retention

  • On your phone: until you uninstall or clear data, or erase saves in the game.
  • Cloud save (Google account): until you delete it (section 11) or delete your Play Games data.
  • Ad and purchase data held by Google: under Google's own retention policies.
  • Emails to support / error reports you send: kept only as long as needed to answer you, and at most 12 months.
  • The developer keeps no other personal data.

11. How to delete your data

  1. Cloud save: in the game open Settings > Cloud Save > Delete cloud save. You can delete only the cloud copy, or the cloud copy and this phone's saves.
  2. This phone: uninstall the game or clear its storage in Android Settings > Apps.
  3. Play Games profile, leaderboard entries, achievements: manage or delete them in the Google Play Games app > Settings, or at https://myaccount.google.com.
  4. Advertising ID: reset or delete it in Android Settings > Privacy > Ads.
  5. Anything else: write to hourglasskingsupport@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data" and I will reply within 30 days. The developer holds no other data about you; purchase records stay with Google Play as the law requires.

12. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU/UK GDPR, Israeli privacy law or the CCPA/CPRA and other US state laws) you may have the right to know what data is processed, access it, correct it, delete it, object to or restrict processing, withdraw consent at any time, and complain to your local data protection authority. Because the developer holds no personal data about you, most of these are done through Google (sections 4 and 11); write to hourglasskingsupport@gmail.com and I will help.

  • GDPR basis: consent (ads, notifications, cloud save), contract (purchases, cloud save you asked for) and legitimate interest (time check, fraud prevention). Google acts as the controller for ads, Play Games and Billing data; the developer is not a processor of that data.
  • CCPA/CPRA: the developer does not sell personal information. Google's use of data for ads may count as "sharing" for targeted advertising under some state laws; limit it with the ad choices in section 4. We do not knowingly sell or share data of anyone under 16.
  • Data may be processed by Google outside your country (including the US), under Google's safeguards.
